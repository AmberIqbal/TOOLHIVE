## Tips for Best Results

Regardless of which tool you choose, follow these tips for optimal background removal:

**1. Use High-Quality Images**
- Minimum 1000px width
- Good lighting
- Clear subject-background separation

**2. Prepare Your Image**
- Crop unnecessary areas
- Ensure subject is in focus
- Avoid cluttered backgrounds

**3. Check the Edges**
- Zoom in to inspect edge quality
- Manually refine if needed
- Use feathering for natural look

**4. Save in Right Format**
- PNG for transparent backgrounds
- JPG if adding new background
- WebP for web optimization
