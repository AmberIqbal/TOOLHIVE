## Tips for Best Background Removal Results

Follow these best practices regardless of which method you choose:

### 1. Start with Quality Images

**Image resolution:**
- Use at least 1000px width for best results
- Higher resolution = better edge detection
- Avoid overly compressed or low-quality images

**Lighting:**
- Good, even lighting on subject
- Avoid harsh shadows on background
- Natural light or studio lighting works best

**Focus:**
- Subject should be in sharp focus
- Blurry edges make AI detection harder
- Use your camera's portrait mode if available

### 2. Choose the Right Background

**Best backgrounds for easy removal:**
- Solid, plain colors
- High contrast with subject
- Uniform lighting
- Minimal texture or patterns

**Difficult backgrounds:**
- Same color as subject
- Busy patterns or multiple objects
- Uneven lighting
- Reflective or glass surfaces

### 3. Prepare Your Image

**Before uploading:**
- Crop out unnecessary elements
- Rotate to correct orientation
- Adjust brightness if needed (slightly brighter = easier detection)

**Position subject:**
- Center your subject in frame
- Include full subject (don't cut off edges)
- Separate subject from background edges

### 4. Check Edge Quality

**After background removal:**
- Zoom in to inspect edges (especially hair, fur)
- Look for leftover background pixels
- Check for overly harsh or soft edges
- Verify transparent areas are fully transparent

**Common edge issues:**
- Halo effect (thin outline around subject)
- Missing fine details (hair strands, fur)
- Rough or jagged edges
- Semi-transparent areas that should be solid

### 5. Save in Correct Format

**PNG format (recommended):**
- Supports transparency
- Lossless quality
- Larger file size
- Best for: Designs, printing, further editing

**JPG format (for web with new background):**
- No transparency support
- Smaller file size
- Some quality loss
- Best for: Web use after adding solid background

**WebP format (modern web):**
- Supports transparency
- Smaller than PNG
- Good quality
- Best for: Website optimization, faster loading

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Problem: Rough or Jagged Edges

**Causes:**
- Low image resolution
- Poor lighting contrast
- Complex background

**Solutions:**
- Use higher resolution source image
- Improve subject-background contrast
- Try different background removal tool
- Manually refine edges in photo editor

### Problem: Missing Hair or Fur Details

**Causes:**
- Very fine details hard for AI to detect
- Similar colors between hair/fur and background
- Backlit subjects

**Solutions:**
- Use images with clear hair/fur separation
- Avoid backlighting when photographing
- Accept some manual cleanup may be needed
- Consider tools specialized for hair/fur

### Problem: Leftover Background Pixels

**Causes:**
- Complex or busy backgrounds
- Reflections or shadows
- Semi-transparent objects

**Solutions:**
- Clean up manually in photo editor
- Use eraser tool for small spots
- Reprocess with cleaner background image
- Take new photo with simpler background

### Problem: Subject Partially Removed

**Causes:**
- Subject blends into background
- Very low contrast
- Unusual subject shapes

**Solutions:**
- Ensure high contrast between subject and background
- Use well-lit images
- Avoid subjects that match background colors
- Retake photo with better separation
